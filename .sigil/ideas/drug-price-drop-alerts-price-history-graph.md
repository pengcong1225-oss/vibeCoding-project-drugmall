# Drug Price Drop Alerts & Price History Graph

## Description

On each drug detail page, patients see a 30/90-day price history line graph showing the drug's price trend on the platform. Below the graph, a "Set Price Alert" button lets patients set a target price. When the drug price drops to or below that target, the patient receives an in-app notification and optional SMS alert. The alert includes the current price, the drop percentage, and a direct link to add to cart. Patients can manage all their price alerts from a central page. Price history data is visible to all users; alerts require login.
