# Health Metrics Self-Tracking Dashboard

## Description

Patients with chronic conditions can log and track key health metrics over time: blood pressure (systolic/diastolic), blood glucose (fasting/postprandial), weight, body temperature, and heart rate. The dashboard displays trend charts (line graphs with target zones), highlights readings outside normal ranges, and correlates metric trends with medication changes (e.g., "since starting Drug X, your average systolic BP dropped 12 mmHg"). Patients can optionally share their dashboard with their doctor via the existing doctor-patient IM channel. AI-generated weekly summaries note patterns and suggest when to consult a doctor.

## Rationale

DrugMall serves chronic disease patients (hypertension, diabetes) who need ongoing monitoring. This bridges the gap between buying drugs and managing health outcomes, increasing platform stickiness. Leverages existing AI and IM infrastructure.
