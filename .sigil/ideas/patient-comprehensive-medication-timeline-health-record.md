# Patient Comprehensive Medication Timeline & Health Record

## Description

A unified, chronologically-organized health timeline that aggregates: all past drug purchases, prescriptions (active and historical), AI consultation transcripts, doctor visit notes, self-reported symptoms, allergy records, and lab/test result uploads. The timeline is filterable by date range, condition category, and drug type. Both the patient (in the patient app) and their authorized doctors (in the doctor app) can view this timeline. Doctors see a summarized view before prescribing. The timeline auto-generates a "health summary" that patients can export as PDF to share with offline doctors. Key events (new diagnosis, new allergy, pregnancy) can be manually added by the patient or auto-detected from prescription patterns.

## Rationale

Currently the system has siloed data: separate prescription records, order history, and AI consultations. A unified timeline creates the "single source of truth" for patient health, improves doctor decision-making, and increases platform stickiness as patients build their health history here.
