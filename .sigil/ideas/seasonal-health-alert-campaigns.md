# Seasonal Health Alert Campaigns

## Description

Admins can create time-bound health alert campaigns triggered by season or public health events: flu season preparedness, allergy season, heat wave, air quality warnings, or infectious disease outbreaks. Each campaign includes: a target region (province/city), health advice content (written + AI-generated), recommended OTC drugs and preventive products from the catalog, and optional discount coupons. Patients in the target region see the campaign banner on the app home screen and receive an optional push notification. Campaign performance metrics (views, clicks, conversions, coupon redemptions) are available in the admin dashboard. Campaigns auto-expire on their end date.

## Rationale

DrugMall's home screen is likely static, missing seasonal engagement hooks. Pharmacies routinely run seasonal campaigns. This drives timely purchases, demonstrates health expertise, and leverages the existing admin, notification, and coupon infrastructure.
