# Verified Purchase Drug Reviews & Ratings

## Description

Patients who have completed a purchase can write a review and rating (1¨C5 stars) for the drug they bought. Reviews are tagged as "Verified Purchase." The review form captures: effectiveness rating, side effects experienced (checkboxes from a predefined list plus free text), ease of use, value for money, and an open-text comment. Reviews are moderated: AI (Baichuan) screens for inappropriate content, medical misinformation, and personally identifiable information before publication. Other patients can mark reviews as "helpful." Each drug detail page shows aggregate star ratings, a side-effect frequency summary, and a "Most Helpful" review. Pharmacists can respond to reviews publicly.

## Rationale

DrugMall sells drugs but patients lack social proof from peers. Real drug experiences (especially side effects) are high-value content that builds trust and aids purchasing decisions. This is standard for pharmacy platforms globally but missing here.
